
ZODIAC WORLD

Zodiac World is an interactive command-line application written in Python. It allows users to calculate their astrological sign based on their birth month and date, view detailed astronomical details (ruling planet, lucky number, element), and read fun personality traits and tailored relationship advice.

HOW TO USE



Make sure Python  is installed on your system.



Interaction Steps:

Indicate whether you want to find your zodiac sign by typing Y or N.

If Y, enter your birth month (e.g., October) and birth date (1-31).

Enter the name of your zodiac sign to explore deeper insights.

Select an option from the menu:

1: View sign details (Ruling Planet, Lucky Number, Element).

2: Read a fun trait/fact about your sign.

3: Get relationship advice tailored to your sign.

Enter Y to continue exploring or any other key to exit the application.
